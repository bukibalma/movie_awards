From IMDb: go to Your Ratings (or any custom list), click the three-dot menu, then Export — it downloads a CSV. Upload it here, or paste the CSV content or a plain list of titles below.

Fully automatic option: drop the exported CSV into the imdb-import/ folder next to docker-compose.yml on your server — the app checks it every 5 minutes and imports on its own, no need to come to this page at all.

This is meant as a one-time backfill. For ongoing tracking, connect Plex on the Settings page — it has a real API, so newly watched movies sync automatically from here on, without needing IMDb at all.
